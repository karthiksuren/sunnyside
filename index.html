The Opportunity

45 cents of land in the middle of Kotagiri town, opening directly onto the Kotagiri–Coonoor main road, with the bus stand, the market and the schools all within walking distance.

Land of this size rarely comes up for sale in the centre of Kotagiri. It is one unbroken plot, mostly level, under a single clear title, and it is being sold whole. On the land are a house over 100 years old, a working well, grown trees including a few fruiting ones, and about 18 cents of tea at the back.

This is being sold for the land, the location and the road frontage. The house is old and needs full renovation. It is shown here exactly as it stands, and buyers should look at it on that basis.

Note on development

All construction in the Nilgiris is regulated by the Hill Area Conservation Authority, and permissible extent is assessed case by case rather than by a published table. Buyers are encouraged to make their own enquiry. The owners will cooperate fully with any pre-purchase feasibility study and will provide survey numbers and revenue records on request.

Position within Kotagiri town. The parcel sits between Coonoor Road and the town centre, minutes on foot from the main bus stand, the market and St. Mary’s.

Why the frontage matters

The parcel fronts the bend where traffic between Kotagiri town and the Coonoor–Ooty road converges. It is one of the most visible and most easily accessed positions in the town, with direct vehicular entry off the main road and no dependence on a private or shared approach.
